The idea

Intensity is easy for a week. Consistency is what changes your body.

Most people don't quit because a workout was too hard. They quit because the plan didn't survive a busy Tuesday, a sore shoulder, or a week away from the gym.

So this app is built around the streak, not the burn. Your plan adjusts to the equipment you actually have and works around the joints that actually hurt. Sessions are short enough to finish. Every one ends with core, because that's the part people skip first — and the part that holds everything else together.

Inside the app

Everything you need. Nothing you'll skip.

Free to start. No card, no setup call, no 40-question quiz — you can skip straight to picking a plan if you want.

ADAPTIVE PLANS

Workouts that fit your life

Four levels, from "I rarely move on purpose" to athlete. Every exercise adjusts to the equipment you own — none, a few dumbbells, or a full gym.

INJURY AWARE

Trains around what hurts

Tap the joints and muscles giving you trouble on a body map. Anything that stresses them gets swapped for something that doesn't.

REQUIRED CORE

Every session ends with core

The finisher isn't optional — the workout won't log until it's done. Swap the movements freely, but you're doing them.

STREAKS & XP

Progress you can see

Streak flames, daily quests, ranks, and badges. Miss a day and a streak freeze has your back. The point is the next session, always.

YOUR REWARDS

Treat yourself, on your terms

Optional: set your own deal. Three days straight earns a sweet treat this weekend, seven earns movie night. You decide the prize.

FOOD & WATER

Meals and hydration, handled

Real recipes with macros, one-tap meal logging, a hydration tracker, and calorie counting that takes five seconds.
